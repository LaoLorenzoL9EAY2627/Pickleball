Pickleball for Starters


Looking to get started with Pickleball? Heres a guide to the Basics:




Rules

  • Games played to 11 points (lead by two), only the serving team can score.
  • Service: must hit the box diagonal to you, clearing the non-volley zone.
  • Non-volley zone: the 'kitchen', you cannot step there when recieving, even landing there after returning the ball is not allowed unless the ball bounces there first.

Techniques

  • Underhand Serve: Hit with an upward motion below the waist to start the point consistently.
  • Dink Shot: Soft, controlled hit from the kitchen that drops over the net to the other kitchen.
  • Drive: Low, fast baseline shot intended to force a mistake and win the point.

Routine

  1. Warmup: Start with light cardio and mobility drills to loosen up your joints and prevent injuries.
  2. Reflexes: Practice kitchen touches with another player, focus on form and footwork.
  3. Service: Practice service and returning with another player to fire up consistency.
  4. Post-Game: Rehydrate, do static stretches and slow breathing to prevent post-game soreness.
